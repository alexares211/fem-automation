CLT Stiffness Optimization

      

        Analytic Classical Laminate Theory — no CalculiX, no mesh. For a
        fixed ply count it assembles every candidate laminate's ABD
        matrix in closed form and ranks by the chosen stiffness measure
        S. Uses the material from section 02 and the ply
        thickness from section 03.
      


      

        

          
          
        

        

          
          
        

      

      

        

          
          
        

        
      

      

        Ply stiffness is 180°-periodic, so the set spans 0–180
        (180 ≡ 0); angles fold into that range, so -45 may be
        typed and becomes 135. Default is 15° steps — widen
        to 30° (0/30/60/90/120/150) for big stacks.
        Single-entry objectives (A11, A22,
        A66, D11) solve layer-by-layer for any N;
        coupled ones (Ex, Ey, Gxy,
        A11+A22) enumerate anglesfree plies
        — keep that under a few ×106 (symmetry halves the exponent).
