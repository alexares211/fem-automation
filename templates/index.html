FEM evaluation history

      
These are orientation-only adaptations. Classical fully stressed sizing targets utilization = 1 by changing material sizing; that target may be unreachable with fixed thickness and ply count. Equal stress adds a balance constraint and can trade stiffness for more uniform utilization. The search reports a local result at the selected resolution and budget.
