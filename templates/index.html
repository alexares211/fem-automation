Material

Orthotropic composite ply properties — written as *Material + *Elastic, Type=Engineering constants.

Model units (moduli in N/mm² = MPa for the TorsionPlatte template). CalculiX order: E1, E2, E3, ν12, ν13, ν23, G12, G13 on line 1, G23 on its own line.
